A LITTLE NOURISHMENT

Good food for hazy days

Comforting, balanced choices to keep you nourished and hydrated.

01 / HYDRATE

Water & warm soup

Keep water on hand and enjoy a warm soup for a comforting, hydrating meal.

02 / FRESH & COLOURFUL

Leafy greens & fresh produce

Enjoy fresh produce as part of a varied diet; it’s nourishing, but not a haze shield.

03 / BALANCED MEAL

A balanced meal with protein

Pair vegetables with protein and whole grains for a satisfying everyday meal.

No food or drink prevents haze exposure or treats its effects. Reduce time outdoors when air quality is poor and follow NEA health advice.
